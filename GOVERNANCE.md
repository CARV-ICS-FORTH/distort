# Governance

DISTORT uses a simple maintainer-led governance model. The lead maintainer
consults the other maintainers and makes the final decision on project matters,
subject to the succession and conflict-of-interest rules below.

## Participation and responsibilities

Anyone may contribute through issues, discussions in pull requests, code,
documentation, and testing. Participants follow the
[Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and [contributor guide](CONTRIBUTING.md).

Maintainers review contributions, maintain releases and documentation, and guide
project direction. The lead coordinates this work. Current maintainers, the
lead, and their contact details are listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md).

## Decisions

- Any maintainer may merge routine fixes and documentation changes after approval
  from another maintainer, including changes authored by the lead.
- For significant changes, releases, governance changes, and unresolved
  disagreements, the lead consults all other maintainers before deciding.
- Maintainers receive a reasonable opportunity to respond, without a fixed
  discussion deadline. The lead considers their views and explains the decision.
- Significant proposals, decisions, and reasoning are recorded in GitHub issues
  or pull requests so contributors can follow and participate in discussions.
- Urgent fixes may proceed immediately, with the other maintainers informed and
  the reasoning recorded afterward. Security-sensitive details remain private
  under the [security policy](SECURITY.md).

## Maintainer membership

Any contributor may express interest in becoming a maintainer. The lead decides
on additions or removals after consulting the other maintainers, considering
sustained contributions, constructive collaboration, and willingness to maintain
the project.

Inactivity or proposed removal is discussed with the affected maintainer first,
making reasonable attempts to contact them if unavailable. Maintainers may step
down voluntarily. Membership changes are recorded in `MAINTAINERS.md`; sensitive
personal details remain private.

## Lead succession

If the lead steps down or becomes unavailable, the remaining maintainers agree
on a successor, consulting the outgoing lead when possible. The change is
recorded publicly and reflected in `MAINTAINERS.md`.

## Conflicts and conduct

Maintainers disclose personal conflicts of interest and leave the affected
decision to an uninvolved maintainer. Nobody decides a conduct complaint about
themselves, including the lead. Reports may be sent privately to an uninvolved
maintainer using the contacts in `MAINTAINERS.md`.
